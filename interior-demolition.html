DCP lists interior demolition among its commercial and industrial interior trades. Each project begins with a defined scope and a review of the available plans and requirements.

Demolition as part of the interior construction scope.

Interior demolition may be included with other trades such as metal stud framing, drywall, acoustical ceilings, painting and related finishes. The requested work and responsibilities are identified through the project scope.

Planning the demolition scope

For a bid review, provide available drawings, the project location, the requested demolition work and relevant site information. Include the bid deadline so the project requirements can be discussed.

Clear plans and a defined scope help move the conversation forward.

Reviewing project requirements

The work included in an interior demolition request depends on the drawings and the project. Material removal, access conditions, scheduling requirements and any related trades should be confirmed during scope review.
